# Privacy

- No account, no analytics, no server of ours. Everything you enter is stored in your
  browser's local storage on your device.
- "Use my location" asks the browser for the device position only when you tap it.
  The position is kept in this browser and sent once to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to
  name your town; it goes nowhere else. Typing your town works the same without it.
- The app sends only what each lookup needs: place-search text to OpenStreetMap
  Nominatim, stop coordinates to the OSRM router, stop coordinates to the National
  Weather Service, and, only if you add your own key, your trip preferences to the
  Anthropic API.
- Your API key stays in this browser and is sent only to api.anthropic.com. Backups
  leave the key out.
- The app never books, reserves, buys, or signs in anywhere. Links open the official
  site so you can book yourself.
