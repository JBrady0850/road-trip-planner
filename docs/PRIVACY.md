# Privacy

- No account, no analytics, no server of ours. Everything you enter is stored in your
  browser's local storage on your device.
- "Use my location" asks the browser for the device position only when you tap it.
  The position is kept in this browser and sent once to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to
  name your town; it goes nowhere else. Typing your town works the same without it.
- The Feedback card keeps your answers in this browser. **Open on GitHub** opens a
  GitHub page with your answers filled into the address; nothing is posted until you
  press Create there, and GitHub's own privacy terms then apply. **Save as a file**
  and **Copy text** keep everything on your device. Your home is never included.
- The app sends only what each lookup needs: place-search text to OpenStreetMap
  Nominatim, stop coordinates to the OSRM router (when you build a trip, also your home
  and the trip's base, to count the drive days), on a drive with an overnight the
  overnight's point on the route to Nominatim once to name the nearest town, stop coordinates to the National
  Weather Service, and, only if you add your own key, your trip preferences to the
  one AI provider you pick in Settings (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, or xAI). A town typed in **City** goes to Nominatim once, and only when the
  catalog does not already have it; catalog towns need no lookup. A port typed on the
  Cruise Day tab goes to Nominatim once, and only when it does not name one of the
  192 listed ports. Excursion, tour, rental, and taxi links open the cruise lines' own pages,
  Viator, GetYourGuide, or a web or map search. A tour or search link carries only
  the port name; the app sends nothing until you tap it.
- Fishing license links open each state's official wildlife agency page. The app
  sends nothing to those sites; you buy the license there yourself.
- The contact email in Settings is optional and is not an account; no sign-up is
  needed anywhere. If you fill it in, it is sent only with place searches and town
  lookups to OpenStreetMap, which asks heavy users to include one.
- Each API key stays in this browser and is sent only to its own provider:
  api.anthropic.com (Claude), api.openai.com (ChatGPT), generativelanguage.googleapis.com
  (Gemini), or api.x.ai (Grok). Only the provider picked in Settings is used. Backups
  leave every key out. Anyone who can use the browser can read a saved key, so save
  one only on your own device.
- With a key, an AI itinerary request carries your home town and its coordinates, your
  start date, nights, party size, interests, driving, walking, and budget limits,
  lodging brand, check-in time, diet, dining limits, and any notes you type. **Make my trip unique** sends your five answers, your interests and walking limit, the area
  being searched (your home or your hotel, with coordinates), and the names of places
  already in the trip or on the list. Without a key, the interview runs on your device
  and sends nothing.
  **Fix the problems with AI** sends the whole trip (the trip name, each day's date and
  title, and every stop's name, address, coordinates, type, times, timed entry, time on
  site, check-in mark, and notes), the plan check's problems except the ones you
  overrode, the measured drive times, your start time, your interests, and your
  driving, check-in, sunset, and walking rules. A drive trip also sends your home town
  and its coordinates; a fly-and-drive trip sends the lodging town, and a cruise port
  day the ship's times instead.
- The app never books, reserves, buys, or signs in anywhere. Links open the official
  site so you can book yourself.
