# Privacy

- No account, no analytics, no server of ours. Everything you enter is stored in your
  browser's local storage on your device.
- "Use my location" asks the browser for the device position only when you tap it.
  The position is kept in this browser and sent once to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to
  name your town; it goes nowhere else. Typing your town works the same without it.
- The Feedback card keeps your answers in this browser. **Open on GitHub** opens a
  GitHub page with your answers filled into the address; nothing is posted until you
  press Create there, and GitHub's own privacy terms then apply. **Save as a file**
  and **Copy text** keep everything on your device. Your home is never included.
- The app sends only what each lookup needs: place-search text to OpenStreetMap
  Nominatim, stop coordinates to the OSRM router, stop coordinates to the National
  Weather Service, and, only if you add your own key, your trip preferences to the
  Anthropic API. A town typed in **City** goes to Nominatim once, and only when the
  catalog does not already have it; catalog towns need no lookup. A port typed on the
  Cruise Day tab goes to Nominatim once, and only when it does not name one of the
  192 listed ports. Excursion, tour, rental, and taxi links open the cruise lines' own pages,
  Viator, GetYourGuide, or a web or map search. A tour or search link carries only
  the port name; the app sends nothing until you tap it.
- Fishing license links open each state's official wildlife agency page. The app
  sends nothing to those sites; you buy the license there yourself.
- The contact email in Settings is optional and is not an account; no sign-up is
  needed anywhere. If you fill it in, it is sent only with place searches to
  OpenStreetMap, which asks heavy users to include one.
- Your API key stays in this browser and is sent only to api.anthropic.com. Backups
  leave the key out.
- The app never books, reserves, buys, or signs in anywhere. Links open the official
  site so you can book yourself.
